TensorDesk

Engineering math for iPhone, iPad and Mac

TensorDesk is a scientific calculator built around worksheets. Type formulas line by line, define variables with units and watch every dependent result update. Exact fractions stay exact; approximate results are clearly marked.

  • Units and conversions, checked in every operation
  • Symbolic algebra, derivatives, integrals and equation solving
  • Matrices, FFT, numerical methods, statistics and finance
  • Inline plots, including Bode diagrams
  • Ready-made formulas and worksheet templates for engineering, physics and finance — try 237 engineering formulas online

Works offline. No account, no tracking, no ads.
