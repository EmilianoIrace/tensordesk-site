Boost duty cycle in DCM

√(2·L·f_sw·I_out·(V_out - V_in))/V_in

Result—

In TensorDesk

Type boost_ddcm(V_in, V_out, I_out, L, f_sw) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: boost_ddcm(12 V, 48 V, 0.05 A, 47 uH, 100 kHz) = 0.3428
