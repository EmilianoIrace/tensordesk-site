Minimum boost inductance for CCM down to I_out

V_in^2·(V_out - V_in)/(2·V_out^2·I_out·f_sw)

Result—

In TensorDesk

Type boost_lcrit(V_in, V_out, I_out, f_sw) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: boost_lcrit(12 V, 48 V, 0.2 A, 100 kHz) = 56.25 µH
