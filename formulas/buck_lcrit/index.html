Minimum buck inductance for CCM down to I_out

(V_in - V_out)·V_out/(2·V_in·I_out·f_sw)

Result—

In TensorDesk

Type buck_lcrit(V_in, V_out, I_out, f_sw) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: buck_lcrit(24 V, 5 V, 0.5 A, 200 kHz) = 19.79 µH
