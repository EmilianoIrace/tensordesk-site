Average DC of a six-pulse bridge (1.35·V_LL·cos α)

3·√(2)/π·V_LL·cos(alpha)

Result—

Ideal bridge in continuous conduction, no commutation overlap; leave α out for diodes.

In TensorDesk

Type vdc6p(V_LL[, alpha]) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: vdc6p(400 V, 30 deg) = 467.8 V
