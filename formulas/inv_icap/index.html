RMS current in the DC-link capacitor of a two-level inverter

I_ph·√(2·m_a·(√(3)/(4·π) + pf^2·(√(3)/π - 9·m_a/16)))

Result—

Kolar's formula; m_a = 2·V̂_ph/V_dc (up to 1.155), sine currents, high switching frequency.

In TensorDesk

Type inv_icap(I_ph, m_a, pf) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: inv_icap(50 A, 0.9, 0.85) = 27.69 A
