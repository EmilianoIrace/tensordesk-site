Three-phase and AC power

Reactive power to compensate for a target power factor

P·(tan(acos(pf_1)) - tan(acos(pf_2)))

Result—

In TensorDesk

Type qcomp(P, pf_1, pf_2) in the calculator or in a worksheet; units are checked and converted for you.

Example: qcomp(30 kW, 0.75, 0.95) = 16.6 kvar
